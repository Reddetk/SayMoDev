## What is in good shape

- ADR-007 boundary is respected: `port/in` and `port/out` DTOs are port-owned; `uuid`, `string`, `time` only; no `core/entity`/`core/valobj` in port signatures.
- Dependency direction is inward; composition root is `cmd/server/main.go`; no global singletons.
- External BC#3 reads are outside the DB transaction in activate, difficulty, complete, resume and consumer flows; outbox writes are in the same TX as state changes; Kafka consumer idempotency is inside the TX (7-day TTL).
- Model B separation, FLOOR percent (`valobj.CalculatePercent`), Monday-first bitmap, IANA timezone and DST `NextUnlock`, `validUntil == nil` indefinite semantics, and the partial unique indexes for one active program/one granted lesson are implemented and unit-tested at the VO/entity level.
- JWT: RS256-only, `kid`, iss/aud/exp, ±30s leeway, JWKS one forced re-fetch and fail-closed, revocation `jti`/`account:rev:` keys matching identy-service; Redis-down → 503. Ownership 404 and admin 403 are wired.
- Readiness (`/health/ready`), request body limit (1 MiB), timeouts, graceful shutdown, migrations with up/down pairs and the deletion-guard index are present; HTTP error mapping matches most documented codes, and `docs/` is regenerated.
